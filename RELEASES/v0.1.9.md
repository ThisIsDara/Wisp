## wisp v0.1.9

The instant app launcher for Windows 10/11. Press Alt+Space anywhere, type a few letters, and launch apps, Store apps, and files.

### What's new

- Spot duplicate apps — when the same program shows up more than once (several Discord entries, say), every copy is now marked and the footer tells you how many there are. Click the count to see them all together
- Remove a copy for real — "Remove" takes a copy out of wisp completely instead of just hiding it, so it doesn't reappear when you switch tabs or retype your search. Nothing is deleted from your PC
- Removals follow your folders — remove a scanned folder from Settings and add it back, and its apps all return, so an accidental removal is never permanent
- Folder list without scrolling — the folders you scan are now chips that wrap onto as many lines as they need, and the Settings window grows to fit. Nothing in Settings scrolls any more
- Scan progress bar out of the way — the bar shown while scanning no longer sits on top of the "last scan" summary or the Scan now button
- Same font on every machine — the search box and app names now use JetBrains Mono everywhere, instead of quietly falling back to a system font where it wasn't installed
- Hide and Unhide work again — they did nothing since an earlier refactor, and now do what they say
