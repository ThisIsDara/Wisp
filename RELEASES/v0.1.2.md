## wisp v0.1.2

The instant app launcher for Windows 10/11. Press Alt+Space anywhere, type a few letters, and launch apps, Store apps, and files.

### What's new

- Fixed "Start with Windows" — wisp now appears in the tray right after login (the tray isn't ready at boot; wisp retries until it is)
- Installer: new "Start with Windows" option (default on) and "Run wisp now" on the finish page
- Settings gear button in the launcher footer — no tray needed to open settings
- Settings window now fades out on close (matching its fade-in)
- Shortcuts (.lnk) are now found when searching files, not just .exe files
- Right-click a result to favorite/unfavorite from the list
- Cleaner result rows (path subtitle removed)

### Install

Download and run [wisp-setup.exe](https://github.com/ThisIsDara/Wisp/releases/download/v0.1.2/wisp-setup.exe)
