## wisp v0.1.6

The instant app launcher for Windows 10/11. Press Alt+Space anywhere, type a few letters, and launch apps, Store apps, and files.

### What's new

- Accent-following look - the launcher's outline, selection ring, and controls now pick up your accent color exactly, and stay legible even with dark or light accents
- Alt+number quick-select - press Alt with a digit while results are showing to launch the 1st-9th result instantly
- `cmd/` typed runner - type `cmd/` followed by any command and run it in a new console, straight from the launcher
- Much faster tab switching - flipping between All and Favorites is now nearly instant, especially on larger lists
- Handles admin-required apps (like Netch) - launching an app that needs administrator rights now elevates and asks for permission automatically, instead of silently refusing to open
- Crash logs - if wisp ever trips up, it now writes a readable crash report to `%APPDATA%\TID\wisp\crash\` so problems can be diagnosed and fixed faster
