## wisp v0.1.5

The instant app launcher for Windows 10/11. Press Alt+Space anywhere, type a few letters, and launch apps, Store apps, and files.

### What's new

- Silky-smooth search - typing no longer stutters. Results are computed in the background and stream onto the screen the moment you pause, so every keystroke feels instant even on a big app library
- Buttery scrolling - long lists stay fluid whether you're browsing your full inventory or reading through results
- Rock-solid icon caching - app icons load once and are kept in memory, so the list no longer re-fetches images every time it updates
- Instant default list - opening the launcher (and clearing your search) now paints the full app list immediately, with no hitch while the background scan refreshes it
